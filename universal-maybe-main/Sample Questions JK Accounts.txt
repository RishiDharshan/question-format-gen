1. Fill in the Blanks Question
According to the ________ Concept, revenue earned during a period should be matched with expenses incurred to earn that revenue.
A. Going Concern B. Matching C. Dual Aspect D. Cost
Correct Answer: B
Detailed Solution:
The Matching Concept requires that expenses related to generating revenue must be recognised in the same accounting period as the related revenue. This helps determine correct profit for the period.
Example: Sales commission for current year sales should be charged in the same year.
Hence, Option B is correct.

2. Statement Based Question
Consider the following statements regarding Accounting Principles:
Statement I: Under the Going Concern Concept, it is assumed that the business will continue for the foreseeable future. Statement II: Because of the Going Concern Concept, fixed assets are normally recorded at liquidation value.
A. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct B. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect C. Statement I is correct, but Statement II is incorrect D. Statement I is incorrect, but Statement II is correct
Correct Answer: C
Detailed Solution:
	•	Statement I is correct: Going Concern assumes the business will continue operations for the foreseeable future. 
	•	Statement II is incorrect: Because of this assumption, assets are generally valued on cost basis less depreciation, not liquidation value. 
Hence, Option C is correct.

3. Journal Entry Concept Based Question
Cash ₹40,000 was introduced into the business by the owner as capital. According to the Rules of Debit and Credit, which of the following journal entries is correct?
A. Cash A/c Dr. ₹40,000 To Capital A/c ₹40,000 B. Capital A/c Dr. ₹40,000 To Cash A/c ₹40,000 C. Cash A/c Dr. ₹40,000 To Sales A/c ₹40,000 D. Drawings A/c Dr. ₹40,000 To Cash A/c ₹40,000
Correct Answer: A
Detailed Solution:
Cash is a Real Account and the rule is Debit what comes in. Since cash comes into the business, Cash A/c is debited.
Capital is a Personal Account representing the owner’s claim, and the rule is Credit the giver. Since the owner gives capital, Capital A/c is credited.
Entry: Cash A/c Dr. ₹40,000     To Capital A/c ₹40,000
Hence, Option A is correct.

4. Journal Entry Concept Based Question
Furniture worth ₹25,000 was purchased for cash. Which of the following entries is correct as per the rules of debit and credit?
A. Cash A/c Dr. ₹25,000 To Furniture A/c ₹25,000 B. Furniture A/c Dr. ₹25,000 To Cash A/c ₹25,000 C. Purchases A/c Dr. ₹25,000 To Cash A/c ₹25,000 D. Furniture A/c Dr. ₹25,000 To Capital A/c ₹25,000
Correct Answer: B
Detailed Solution:
Furniture is a Real Account. Since furniture comes in, debit the asset received.
Cash is also a Real Account. Since cash goes out, credit Cash A/c.
Entry: Furniture A/c Dr. ₹25,000     To Cash A/c ₹25,000
Hence, Option B is correct.
